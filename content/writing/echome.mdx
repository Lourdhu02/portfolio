## What it is

ECHOME is a digital twin that runs entirely on your own machine. It learns how you think through an adaptive personality assessment, sounds like you through a local voice clone, and acts for you through an agent that remembers past conversations.

- **Code**: [github.com/Lourdhu02/echome](https://github.com/Lourdhu02/echome)
- **Stack**: Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, Qdrant, MiniLM embeddings, Ollama, Whisper, Resemblyzer, XTTSv2, HiFi-GAN, Docker.

## Memory that is tested, not assumed

Chat history in a context window is not memory. ECHOME uses a CoALA-style design with three tiers: episodic memories in Qdrant with recency-decayed retrieval, semantic facts consolidated from those episodes, and procedural patterns mined from how you interact.

The repo includes a multi-session evaluation (`python -m tests.eval.run_eval`). A fact is planted in one session and probed later, between 2 and 52 turns afterward:

- **With memory**: the needed fact is recalled in 11 of 12 scenarios (**92%**).
- **With memory off**: **0%**.
- **Retrieval**: under 1 ms with 200 episodes stored.

## An assessment that asks less

The personality test uses Item Response Theory: a Graded Response Model with MAP estimation, choosing each next question to maximize Fisher information at your current trait estimate. It cuts test length by **70%** while keeping standard error under 0.32 across 8 trait dimensions.

Speech runs locally too: Whisper for speech-to-text, Resemblyzer speaker embeddings, and XTTSv2 with HiFi-GAN for the voice.
