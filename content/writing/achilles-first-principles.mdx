## Why Achilles?

Most AI education falls into one of two extremes: high-level library API wrappers (`model.generate()`) or dense academic papers with no runnable code. 

**Achilles** (`github.com/Lourdhu02/achilles`) was built to bridge this gap: an open-source, test-driven curriculum where you implement every fundamental primitive of modern frontier models from scratch, validated against 229 automated unit tests in CI across Linux, macOS, and Windows.

## The 18 Test-Driven Labs

The curriculum progresses through the entire deep learning stack:

1. **Autograd Engine**: Reverse-mode automatic differentiation on multidimensional tensors with dynamic DAG tape.
2. **Llama-Style Transformer**: RMSNorm, SwiGLU activations, and Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE).
3. **FlashAttention in OpenAI Triton**: Custom GPU fused forward and backward attention kernels, bypassing intermediate HBM roundtrips.
4. **KV Cache & Generation**: Paged attention and continuous batching primitives.
5. **LoRA & Parameter-Efficient Tuning**: Low-rank matrix decomposition with rank scaling $\alpha / r$.
6. **Alignment (DPO & GRPO)**: Direct Preference Optimization and Group Relative Policy Optimization loss functions.
7. **Quantization**: INT8 vector-quantized matrix multiply and FP8 scaling factors.
8. **Speculative Decoding**: Draft-target verification loops achieving 2.2× decode speedups.
9. **Mixture of Experts (MoE)**: Top-k expert routing with load-balancing auxiliary loss.

## Continuous Integration & Test Suite

Every lab comes with strict unit tests (`pytest`). No PR merges without passing all 229 tests verifying numerical precision against reference PyTorch and Hugging Face implementations down to $10^{-6}$ tolerance.
