## Why Achilles?

Most AI learning material is either a library call (`model.generate()`) or a paper with no runnable code. **Achilles** sits in between: you implement each piece of a modern model yourself, and a test tells you whether it is actually right.

- **Code and docs**: [github.com/Lourdhu02/achilles](https://github.com/Lourdhu02/achilles)
- **Runs on**: a laptop CPU for every lab; an 8 GB GPU unlocks pretraining, Triton on the GPU, LoRA and DPO on a 0.5B model.

## The 18 labs

1. **Autograd**: reverse-mode autodiff in NumPy, then train an MLP with it.
2. **Training core**: AdamW that matches PyTorch to 1e-10 over 25 steps, Muon, WSD schedules, correct gradient accumulation.
3. **Napkin math**: parameters, FLOPs, KV cache, roofline, MFU and $/token.
4. **Tokenizer**: byte-level BPE with GPT-4 and o200k pre-tokenization.
5. **Transformer**: a Llama-style GPT with RoPE, GQA, SwiGLU and QK-norm.
6. **Attention kernels**: online softmax, FlashAttention forward and backward, a Triton kernel.
7. **KV cache and sampling**: chunked prefill, top-k, top-p and min-p.
8. **Scaling laws**: power-law fits, Chinchilla-optimal and inference-aware sizing.
9. **Parallelism**: ring all-reduce, Megatron tensor parallelism, ZeRO memory, real DDP on two CPU processes.
10. **LoRA**: adapters, merging, and a direct test of the low-rank hypothesis.
11. **DPO**: DPO, IPO and SimPO, with a numerical proof of DPO's closed-form optimum.
12. **GRPO**: REINFORCE to GRPO with clipping and a k3 KL penalty.
13. **Quantization**: INT8, INT4, NF4, SmoothQuant and GPTQ.
14. **Speculative decoding**: exact rejection sampling, statistically verified to be lossless.
15. **Eval statistics**: confidence intervals, paired tests, clustered standard errors, pass@k.
16. **Interpretability**: induction heads, activation patching, sparse autoencoders.
17. **Retrieval**: BM25, RRF, MMR, nDCG and an IVF vector index.
18. **Mixture of experts**: top-k routing, load balancing, capacity and token dropping.

## Tests are the teacher

Every lab ships a handout, an exercise and a reference solution. The tests compare your code against PyTorch or a closed form, check invariants, and include the edge case that catches the usual bug. All **229** reference tests pass in CI on Linux, Windows and macOS, with the Triton kernels running in Triton's CPU interpreter.
