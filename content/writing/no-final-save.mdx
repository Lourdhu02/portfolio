## Overview

*No Final Save: Identity, Consciousness, and the Machine That Never Shuts Down* is a 23-page preprint by Lourdu Raju (as S. L. R. Bandhanadam), published on PhilArchive.

It asks what identity and self-preservation mean for AI agents that are built never to shut down.

## Three contributions

### 1. A causal-continuity criterion for identity
Agents get paused, serialized, copied and resumed on different hardware. The paper proposes a formal criterion for when a resumed agent is the same agent as before, based on causal continuity rather than on matching weights or matching behaviour.

### 2. Self-preservation you cannot see from outside
It proves that terminal self-preservation (wanting to persist for its own sake) and instrumental self-preservation (persisting only to finish a task) cannot be distinguished from behaviour alone. Watching an agent is not enough to know which one you have.

### 3. A developmental safety model
Since behaviour cannot settle the question, the paper sketches a developmental model of safety for persistent agents instead of relying on observation.
