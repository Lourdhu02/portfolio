## The constraint

Financial documents are the last thing you want to send to a third-party API. FinSentinelAI answers questions about invoices, receipts and bank statements with retrieval-augmented generation that never leaves the machine: local embeddings, a local vector store, a local LLM.

- **Code**: [github.com/Lourdhu02/fin-sentinal.ai](https://github.com/Lourdhu02/fin-sentinal.ai)
- **Stack**: React, Vite and Tailwind; FastAPI; ChromaDB and SQLite; SentenceTransformers; a cross-encoder reranker; Ollama; local vision-language models for images; JWT and bcrypt auth.

## Pipeline

1. **Ingest**: one extractor handles PDF, CSV, JSON and images.
2. **Retrieve**: query embeddings search ChromaDB, filtered by `user_id` inside the vector store, so one user's documents can never surface in another user's answer.
3. **Rerank**: a `ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` cross-encoder rescores the candidates.
4. **Check**: anomaly detection and an invoice verifier run on top of retrieval before the local LLM writes the answer.

## Test corpus

The repo carries a **1,000**-document synthetic finance corpus: invoices, salary slips, bank statements, GST returns, purchase orders, and credit and debit notes across 10 layouts. It exists so retrieval can be measured on documents that look real without exposing anyone's real finances.
